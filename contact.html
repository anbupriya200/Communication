❓ Frequently Asked Questions FAQ
Are FluentPath band scores official IELTS certificates? ▾
No. All scores and diagnostic rubrics on FluentPath are practice simulations designed to benchmark your current preparation level. Official IELTS test score reports (TRF) can only be awarded by taking the exam at an authorized British Council, IDP, or Cambridge Assessment testing center.
How does native audio pronunciation work? ▾
FluentPath uses the browser's built-in Web Speech API (SpeechSynthesis) to voice vocabulary cards and conversation dialogue lines in natural British and international English accents. Make sure your browser audio is unmuted.
Can I retake full-length mock exams? ▾
Yes, absolutely! You can retake the mock exams as many times as you like. Your latest test results, accuracy rates, and completed test counts are automatically synchronized to your progress tracker.
How is my daily study streak maintained? ▾
Completing any learning activity—such as learning 5 vocabulary flashcards, doing a 1-minute speaking challenge, or submitting a quiz—records activity for that day and extends your streak multiplier.
Does the portal support both Light and Dark mode? ▾
Yes! You can toggle between dark and light themes at any time by clicking the sun/moon icon in the navigation bar. Your theme choice is automatically saved and synchronized across all pages.
