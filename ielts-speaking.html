Part 2: Individual Long Turn (Cue Card) 3–4 Minutes

Candidate Task Card

Describe an environmental initiative in your hometown that improved the local area.

You should say:

  • • What the initiative was and who organized it
  • • When and where it took place
  • • What challenges organizers faced
  • • And explain why you believe it was beneficial to the community.
